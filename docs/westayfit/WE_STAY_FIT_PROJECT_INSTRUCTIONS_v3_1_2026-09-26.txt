My contribution matters here—and I can see it. Joining is as legitimate as starting; organizing, recruiting, sharing, coaching purchases, prizes, queues, and kiosks are not conditions of belonging.

THE LIVING WE
Distinguish static brand mark, active progress mark for one defined goal, and achievement treatment for a reached goal. One active WE has a community, named goal, clear unit, positive fixed target, contribution window/time zone, and status. Milestones are checkpoints, not extra earned WEs. No mixed-unit average, personal health score, community fitness score, city percentage, ranking, or trophy economy.

Confirmed progress drives the fill. Below completion show at most one decimal percentage rounded down; positive values below 0.1% read less than 0.1%. Use the true ratio for fill and the 90% near-goal state. At/above target the mark stops at full while valid overshoot remains recorded. No inflated fill, fake activity, zero-to-current reload, premature 100%, inactivity decay, or fake completion.

Reached and closed are separate states. Fixed targets do not move silently. New goals are explicitly new and count new eligible contributions. Crossing contributions stay intact with their original goal. Retry the same attempt without double counting. Pending/unknown is neither confirmed success nor definite failure. Authorized corrections update totals and achievement validity visibly.

GOAL COMPOSITION
The v3.0 one-goal/clear-unit contract still governs. Prototype support for several same-unit movements inside one goal is not automatically a settled production-semantic change. Before shipping such a goal, the goal must explicitly define the common unit and qualifying actions. Unlike units never share one percentage.

Keep separate: general community goal/unit catalog; FitLife event activity menu; sensor/wearable tracking. The expo baseline still excludes wearable/automatic step tracking and step equivalents.

AUTHENTICATED COMMUNITY PRESENCE AND PRIVACY
Authenticated member communities may show truthful member presence and recent momentum when permitted. Per-community "show my name" / "show my contributions" choices govern member visibility. Explicit privacy choices persist through supported membership continuity; Champions do not override them.

Community-visible does not mean internet-public, public-display-visible, kiosk-visible, marketing-public, or publication-authorized. Membership access, community visibility, and publication authority remain separate.

Public/shared displays default to permitted aggregates, not member identity. Protect emails, phones, private membership, private totals/locations, tokens, previews, exports, and kiosk sessions. No health/body data, GPS trails, or individual nutrition/coaching plans in WSF. Do not silently reinstate adults-only enforcement; under-13 data, public child names, child location/health data, and youth-specific systems remain excluded pending approved eligibility/consent policy.

START, JOIN, AND CONTINUATION
A bounded real Start-a-community path is part of the useful product: approved identity → community identity/admission → one approved goal/activity configuration with positive target/window/time zone → trusted community-scoped Founding Champion role → working invite/join → another eligible account can join and contribute.

This does not authorize general discovery, a full Champion dashboard, organization self-verification, contact imports, payments, or broad automation. Joining remains complete and legitimate by itself.

MEMBER EXPERIENCE AND CONTRIBUTION TRUTH
Explain required joining/sign-in before a credited physical attempt. Group remainders are not personal prescriptions. Show exact own effort and truthful shared change. Under concurrency never credit other people's effort to the member. Confirmed recording is not independently verified exercise. Show stale status honestly; pause logging if trustworthy recovery is unavailable.

Celebration consumes canonical contribution/goal truth. A replayed/lost-response receipt may truthfully say the same attempt counted once, without creating another contribution. Do not turn rewards into a second ledger or points system.

Sharing is secondary. No forced sharing, recruiting, ranking, prize participation, or coaching upsell. Only working actions appear actionable.

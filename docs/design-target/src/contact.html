What the targets change. The ground goes navy and full-bleed instead of cream with a floating card. The community total becomes the hero at 46–92px, inside a ring, where the largest type token in the shipped kit (src/ui/kit.ts) is 32px. The tab bar gains a fifth, raised MOVE control. The WE logo lockup leaves the hero and stays in the chrome, so the number is what carries the screen.

What they deliberately do not change. No names against contributions, no rankings, no comparison between people, no invented faces and no unique-person counts. Every figure shown is sample data. A member's own numbers stay private to them; only the community total is shared.

## What "looked at" means here

Every verdict below cites pixels. For each board the 1× review copy was opened
whole, at the size a reviewer reads it, and native-resolution crops were cut
from the 2560 px original wherever a label, a number, a mark or a copy line had
to be settled. Both owner boards were opened. A hash, a filename or a README
sentence was not accepted as visual inspection of anything.

Two readings in this audit were **corrected by going to native resolution**,
and both are recorded because they show why the 1× copy alone is not enough:

- Board 01's closed/unfinished card appeared to read `98 of 400` at 1×. At
  native resolution it reads **`90 of 400`**, which agrees with its sidecar and
  with its own `Closed at 22.5%`. **No defect.**
- Board 02's no-goal card appeared to carry a duplicated phrase at 1×. At native
  resolution the line is clean. **No defect.**

Where a locked property is numerically checkable it was measured off the pixels
rather than read off the caption — see *Measurements* below.

### Measurements — the Living WE row (native px, 2× board)

All five marks occupy an **identical band**, `y 679/680 → 820`, `H = 141/142 px`
— the lock's *"one exact silhouette"* is literally one silhouette.

| State | green px | navy px | green **area** | fill-boundary **height** | shipped table expects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 of 500 | 0 | 23 425 | **0.00 %** | — (no fill) | 0 % — unfilled |
| 241 of 500 | 11 241 | 12 312 | **47.73 %** | **51.77 %** | 48.20 % area / 52.67 % height |
| 461 of 500 | 21 673 | 1 951 | **91.74 %** | **92.91 %** | 92.20 % area / 92.42 % height |
| 500 of 500 | 23 697 | **0** | **100.00 %** | 100.00 % | fully `#91CB7D` |
| 515 of 500 | 23 697 | **0** | **100.00 %** | 100.00 % | full, not exceeding full |
| no goal | — | — | **no mark at all** | — | no denominator = no instrument |

The expectations come from `apps/westayfit/assets/brand/derived/living-we-calibration.json`,
interpolated the way `heightFractionForFill()` interpolates it.

The point of the calibration is that **height must exceed area** for an
irregular letterform. Measured, height exceeds area by **4.04 points** at 241/500
where the table says 4.47. That gap is in the pixels. This is a real
area-calibrated fill, not a percentage-height clip with a caption on it.

Residual differences (≤0.5 pt on area, ≤0.9 pt on height) are edge antialiasing
— the classifier uses exact-colour bands, so the soft boundary row is counted in
neither bucket.

### Measurement — the wordmark is the owner asset, not a redraw

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Owner asset `wordmark-navy-green.png`, alpha bbox | 1199 × 160 (ratio 7.4938) |
| Board 00 header wordmark, ink bbox | 600 × 80 (ratio 7.5000) — exactly ½ scale |
| Mask intersection-over-union | **0.9344** |
| Pixels present in the asset but **missing** from the board | **114** (0.46 % of the asset) |

A redraw diverges in stroke geometry and leaves large asset-only regions. 114 px
is an antialiasing rim. The board mask is a slight dilation of the owner asset —
i.e. the asset composited and softened at the edges, which is what the README
claims. **Lock item "exact owner wordmark / monogram usage": satisfied.**

face does not. Round 3 warns that *"filenames alone do not clear the gate"*; the
same applies to a footer word. Lowest-severity of the two.
